AN EXPERIMENT IN PROOF OF LIFE

What if proof of work could grow something?

On Squeekchain, a real hamster's wheel generates work recorded on Ethereum. Here, that data becomes a forest. Every 60 settled Squeek blocks grows another pixel tree. When a grove completes, another chapter begins.

Wind and firefly motion respond to the wheel only when the hardware feed is fresh. If data is stale, simulated, or unavailable, the forest stays still and tells you instead of pretending to be live.
